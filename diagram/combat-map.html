RED NIGHT v9 · combat map

How an enemy decides to shoot you, what happens to the bullet, and how the recorded gun sounds got from ElevenLabs into the game.

Enemy brain → bullet

HearingEvery shot is a noise event: yours carries 75 m (16 m suppressed), theirs 45 m.Gunfire pulls monsters and gangs toward a fight they couldn't see.
alerts + a fuzzy "last known" spot · rounds passing within 1.8 m add suppression
SightChecked every 0.25–0.35 s: 120° cone, 45 m unaware / 70 m alert, ×0.7 if you crouch, line of sight through walls and cars.You can sneak past, and cover actually hides you. First sighting adds a 0.45–0.85 s reaction delay.
aware or not · forgets you after 14 s out of sight
Squad directorEvery 0.6 s, aware gunmen get roles: 2 nearest suppress, the rest flank wide, melee rushers arc in, anyone under 32% HP retreats.So a group fights as a group instead of five people running at you.
role per enemy
Purger brainFinds cover behind cars and crates, crouches, peeks to fire in bursts, ducks when suppressed, abandons flanked cover, hunts round buildings by street intersections, throws molotovs if you hide.The tactical layer.
Monster brainUnaware: wander or hunt civilians. Aware: their own attacks (leap, dash, spit, hex), tracking your last known spot.They feel like predators, not turrets.
gate: sees you · peeking · reaction done · under 55 m
Physical bulletA real ray with spread from range, your speed, slides, their suppression and movement. First shot goes wider.Replaces the old dice-roll hit chance, so walls and cars stop bullets.
first thing the ray touches
Wall / streetSurface-specific hole, dust, sparks and sound (brick, stone, plaster, wood, glass storefront, metal shutter, asphalt, curb).Instanced: hundreds of holes in about 8 draw calls.
CarHoles that move with the car, glass breaks on the 2nd hit, smoke under 60%, hood fire under 30% that cooks off, then a blast that hurts everyone within 9 m and chains to nearby cars.Cars are cover until they aren't.

Gun sound pipeline

ElevenLabs Sound Effects34 prompts × 4 variants (11 guns, 10 foley, 4 impact types, ricochet, glass, car blast, fire, whiz, scream, distant fire).Recorded-sounding sources that synthesis can't match.
captured in the page → 1 file, red-night-sfx.json (5.5 MB) to Downloads
Device bridgeStaged from Downloads into the build machine.Avoided ~130 separate downloads.
136 Opus clips
sfxpackScores each variant (early single transient wins), cuts guns at any second report, trims silence, fades, normalizes, encodes mono MP3.A one-shot sample has to be exactly one shot.
36 MP3s (660 KB) · .45 and .50 dropped as multi-shot, replaced by pitched 9mm / sniper
In gameSample + street slapback or room reverb; distant enemy fire is low-passed by range. Any missing sample falls back to the layered synth.Nothing goes silent if a file fails to load.

City pipeline (v14)

Sources (all CC0)ambientCG photo facades (9 used), Poly Haven scanned props (21), Tripo people simplified to ~9k triangles for crowds.Real-world detail without hand modelling.
facades: lit offices generated from the glass mask · props: cut to 600-3k triangles, 256-512 px textures
City buildNormal buildings get a photo facade by district; tall towers become one of 5 skyscraper designs (setback, octagon, round, twisted, art deco). About 5,600 props, 1,000 mailboxes and 8,300 grass tufts are placed along wider sidewalks, in alleys and in parks.Fills the open streets.
gate: each prop drawn only within 24-55 m and not well behind the camera, refilled 5 times a second
CrowdsGroups of 1-5 walk a loop around each block, turn or cross at corners, wait for cars, and stop to talk. 28 people on low and 46 on high. A death nearby makes everyone run.People move like people.
Traffic7 cars on low and 12 on high drive the right-hand lane, turn at intersections, brake for people, honk and floor it at gunfire. Any of them can be carjacked. 42 parked cars.Things moving up and down the streets.
